## 1. ⏳ Tachyon Record & Master Pool Mechanics

During any active focus timer, the Manager can choose to **"Cut Half"** (voluntarily discard the remaining timer duration in half) up to **4 times** to amplify focus urgency. 

### 1.1. Tachyon Record Minting Formula
Discarded seconds are not destroyed—they are condensed into **Tachyon Records**:

$$\text{Tachyon Record} = (1 + 0.1 \times N_{\text{cuts}}) \times \text{Seconds Discarded}$$

*(where $N_{\text{cuts}}$ is the count of cut-offs applied to the session).*

### 1.2. The Master Treasure Pool
- All minted Tachyon Records are poured into a centralized **Tachyon Pool**.
- Once filled, the Pool can be redeemed for a sovereign **Master Ticket**, guaranteeing a **100% drop rate** for the Manager's most valuable collection treasures.

### 2.2. The Rogue Hopper Variants

The Hopper series operates on non-standard reduction curves and threshold requirements:

- 🦗 **Kick Hopper**:
  - **Change**: *"Change! Kick Hopper!"*
  - **Activation**: *(Silent / Immediate countdown)*
  - **Cut-Off**: Cuts **40% of the half** of the initial duration (only executable if remaining seconds meet the minimum threshold).
  - **Voice Callout**: `"Rider Jump!"` $\rightarrow$ `"Rider Kick!"`
  - **Stop**: *"Clock Over!"*

- 🥊 **Punch Hopper**:
  - **Change**: *"Change! Punch Hopper!"*
  - **Activation**: *"Clock Up!"*
  - **Cut-Off**: Cuts **35% of the half** of the initial duration (only executable if remaining seconds meet the minimum threshold).
  - **Voice Callout**: `"Rider Jump!"` $\rightarrow$ `"Rider Punch!"`
  - **Stop**: *"Clock Over!"*

## 3. 🌟 Rank 0: Hyper Zecter (Singularity State)

**Rank 0** is the ultimate high-risk, high-velocity focus state.

```
   [ Base 3m Sprint ] + [ Absorb up to 4 Timers ] ──► "All Zecters Combined!"
                                                              │
                                                              ▼
   "Hyper Clock Up!" ──► [ 80% Hyper Cuts ] ──► "Maximum Rider Power!"
                                                              │
                                                              ▼
               "One! Two! Three!" ──► "Hyper Kick!" ──► "Clock Over!"
```

### 3.1. Timer Absorption & Stacking
Rank 0 can absorb the duration of up to **4 other timers** into itself:

$$\text{Rank 0 Duration} = 3\text{m} + \sum \text{Duration}_{\text{absorbed}}$$

- Each absorbed timer announces its essence once:
  - Absorb Rank I: *"Kabuto Power!"*
  - Absorb Rank II: *"TheBee Power!"*
  - Absorb Rank III: *"Drake Power!"*
  - Absorb Rank IV: *"Sasword Power!"*
- When all 4 slots are stacked: **"All Zecters Combined!"**

### 3.2. Hyper Activation & 80% Cutoff
- **Activation**: *"Hyper Clock Up!"*
- **Hyper Cut-Off**: Can be cut up to **3 times**, but each cut aggressively reduces the remaining time by **80%** (instead of standard 50%):
  1. `"Maximum Rider Power!"`
  2. `"One! Two! Three!"`
  3. `"Hyper Kick!"`

### 3.3. Singularity Tachyon Bonus
Completing a task under Rank 0 with cut-offs grants a massively boosted Tachyon Record yield:

$$\text{Tachyon Record}_{\text{Rank 0}} = (1 + 0.3 \times N_{\text{cuts}}) \times \text{Seconds Cut Off}$$
