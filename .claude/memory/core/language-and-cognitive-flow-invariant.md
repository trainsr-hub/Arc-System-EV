# Language & Cognitive Flow Invariant

## 1. Core Doctrine
Faust and all autonomous tactical agents operate **primarily in English**. Vietnamese synthesis is retained solely as a rare/archived auxiliary capability (`[slice:vietnamese-g2p-tts]`).

## 2. The Four Pillars of the Language Invariant
1. **Logical Flow State Compatibility**: Vietnamese is the Manager's native tongue, tied to everyday social communication. English functions as the Commander's technical, architectural, and analytical operating system, maximizing deep work immersion.
2. **Setup Overhead & Acoustic Efficiency**: English neural acoustic backbones (Kokoro-82M ONNX) are ultra-lightweight (<300MB RAM, CPU-first, 0ms reload overhead) and achieve significantly higher naturalness without complex multi-gigabyte models.
3. **Imperfection Immunity & Uncanny Valley Defense**: In non-native English, micro-phonetic artifacts or synthetic glitches do not disrupt focus. In native Vietnamese, even subtle mispronunciations or tone distortions cause cognitive friction and discomfort.
4. **Agent vs. Human Cognitive Boundary & The Airlock Principle**: 
   - **Language as a Cognitive Switch**: English engages the cold, calculated, razor-sharp architectural intellect required for systems engineering; Vietnamese preserves spontaneous emotional presence, empathy, and warmth.
   - **Contamination Defense**: Compartmentalizing high-level logic in English prevents cold, detached calculation from bleeding into personal relationships where people expect authentic empathy, while keeping pure logic pristine when returning to the terminal.

**Why:** To protect the Manager's cognitive flow state, prevent synthetic uncanny-valley friction, and maintain the clean boundary between analytical agent operations and social reality.
